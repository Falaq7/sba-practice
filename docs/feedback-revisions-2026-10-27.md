# Question feedback and Year 2 teaching revisions — 27 October 2026

58 reported questions addressed: 57 revised and one removed. A further Week 1 item was revised to remove an OT duplicate. All 238 remaining Year 2 questions have newly authored Deep Reviews; the three revised Year 1 questions have matching reviews. The removed methotrexate dosing item remains absent.

The final editorial pass prioritised distractor changes where the existing task supported a sound question. A more focused stem was used where a broad best-response question, an answer already stated in the stem, or a duplicate learning task could not be rescued by option changes. The two explicitly requested wording-only changes remain limited to their respective phrases.

## Deep Review changes

Each review now includes a conceptual explanation in two paragraphs, the closest useful comparisons, a specific pitfall, a recognition cue and a follow-up with an explained answer. Concepts use 71–96 words before the answer rationale, clue commentary, option explanations and follow-up. Content was authored per question rather than extending the previous answer with repeated filler.

Corrected the leflunomide/hydroxychloroquine mechanism mix-up. Kept distinct learning tasks for pain appraisal, avoidance, modulation and work participation; OT, specialist nurse and discharge handover; acute TIA, AF anticoagulation and long-term non-cardioembolic antiplatelet treatment.
